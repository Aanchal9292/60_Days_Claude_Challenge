🚀 Day 33/60 of #ABTalksAIChallenge

Can a headline completely change how we perceive a story?

Today I built an AI-powered Headline Reality Check that compares a news headline with the actual article to identify whether the headline accurately represents the content or exaggerates it for clicks.

The application analyzes the relationship between a headline and its article, then highlights where the two don't match.

🔍 Key Features:

✅ Headline Accuracy Score – Measures how closely the headline reflects the article.

✅ Highlighted Mismatches – Detects misleading words like "Secretly" or "Overnight" and explains why they're inaccurate.

✅ Context-Based Explanation – Provides evidence from the article instead of just giving a score.

✅ Source Reliability Section – Designed to evaluate the credibility of the information source.

✅ Emotional Manipulation Detection – Identifies emotionally charged language that can influence readers.

✅ Audience Targeting Analysis – Shows how certain headlines may be crafted to appeal to specific audiences.

💡 What I Learned

This project reminded me that misinformation isn't always about false facts—it often begins with how the story is presented.

A few carefully chosen words can create urgency, fear, or curiosity, even when the article itself tells a very different story.

Building this helped me better understand:

Natural Language Processing (NLP)
AI-based text comparison
Explainable AI (XAI)
UI design for AI applications
Presenting AI insights in a way users can easily understand

Every project in this challenge is helping me explore how AI can be used to make information more transparent and trustworthy.
