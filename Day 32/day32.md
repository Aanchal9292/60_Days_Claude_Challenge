Past the halfway mark! 🎉

Today's project: I built an interactive Marketing Strategy Simulator that turns marketing strategy into a hands-on decision-making experience.

Instead of just reading marketing tips, users actually think like a strategist.

🔹 Every session lets you choose your starting point:
🏢 Use your own real business
🙋 Build your personal brand (name, expertise, and story — no company needed)
🎲 Get a randomly generated client with its own industry, audience, budget, and challenge

Then comes the real work — making the decisions a strategist actually makes:

🧠 Understanding the audience
📱 Choosing the right platforms (and explaining why others don't fit)
🧱 Picking 3 content pillars out of several — each one tied to a different goal
🗓️ Planning a 30-day roadmap by weekly strategic goals, not daily posts
⚡ Reacting to an unexpected event — viral moment, copycat, podcast invite — and seeing the consequences of the choice

What made this project interesting was realizing that marketing isn't about generating content — it's about making trade-offs on purpose. The "best" platform isn't the trendiest one, it's the one your specific audience already uses. The "best" content pillar isn't the most fun to make, it's the one serving the goal you actually need right now.

💡 What I learned while building:

✅ Designing multi-path user flows (business vs. personal brand vs. randomized client) with shared components
✅ Managing branching state with React's useState across an 8+ step journey
✅ Writing mode-aware data generators instead of hardcoded content, so one component adapts across very different contexts
✅ Building consequence-driven interactions where each user choice changes the final output
✅ Embedding "How to ask Claude" prompt cards throughout — teaching prompt engineering alongside the subject itself
✅ Designing a dark, distraction-free UI with CSS variables, custom typography pairing, and smooth transitions — no frameworks, single file, fully offline

At the end of the simulation, users get a personalized Growth Report showing:

📊 Audience Understanding
📱 Platform Strategy
🧱 Content Strategy
📈 Growth Potential
✅ Best Decision Made
⚠️ Biggest Risk to Watch
🎓 Three Marketing Lessons — tailored differently for a business vs. a personal brand

This project reminded me that the best learning tools don't just explain a concept — they make you make the decision yourself, and then show you why it mattered.
