Project: Customer & MVP Blueprint Generator

Yesterday my AI told me my startup idea already has 12+ competitors.

Today I asked it a harder question: "Okay, so what should I actually build first?"

Most founders skip straight from "validated idea" to "let's build the whole product." I wanted a tool that forces the uncomfortable middle step — turning a validation report into a brutally specific MVP scope, before a single line of code gets written.

What I Built:
✅ A Customer & MVP Blueprint generator — takes a Startup Validation Report and extracts ICP, buyer persona, pain points, customer journey, objections, and buying triggers
✅ A MoSCoW-prioritized MVP scope — explicit "Must Have" vs. "Won't Have (v1)" so scope creep gets killed before it starts
✅ Scored outputs (Customer Clarity, Problem Severity, PMF Potential, MVP Readiness) — numbers instead of vague confidence
✅ A 30-day MVP plan + a Top 10 Founder Action Sheet — the report ends in actions, not just analysis

Key Insight:
The most valuable line in the whole report wasn't the "Must Have" list — it was the "Won't Have (v1)" list. Knowing what NOT to build first is what actually protects a founder's next 30 days.

What's Next:
Turning tomorrow's build toward stress-testing this MVP scope against a real pricing model — can the "institutional cohort" wedge actually cover its own AI API costs at scale?

Validation tells you if an idea is worth pursuing. This is the step nobody builds a tool for: turning "yes, pursue it" into "here's exactly what to build first."
