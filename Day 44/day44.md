Day 44/60 — #ABTalksAIChallenge

I let AI roast my own LinkedIn profile. It wasn't gentle.

Today's build: an interactive LinkedIn Roast & Rebuild tool — a system that scores every section of a profile the way a recruiter actually reads it in 3 seconds, then rewrites it and hands you a 7-day activation plan.

I ran it on myself first.

What I Built
✅ A scoring engine that grades Headline, About, Experience, and Skills — not on vibes, but on what each section is actually costing you in recruiter search visibility
✅ A full section-by-section rewrite: 3 headline options, a restructured About (hook → story → proof → CTA), rebuilt Experience bullets, and prioritized skill additions
✅ A before/after scorecard and a 7-day plan with drafted posts and connection templates, not just "update your profile" advice
✅ A shareable summary card built for screenshotting

Key Insight
My biggest gap wasn't a weak sentence — it was a missing section. My real internship work at UptoSkills (database migration, an idempotent seeding script, actual code reviews) wasn't listed as an Experience entry at all. It existed. It just wasn't visible. The lesson: sometimes the highest-leverage fix isn't better writing, it's making real work show up where it's supposed to.

Score before: 38/100. After the rebuild: 80/100. Same person, same work — just finally legible to the people looking for it.

What's Next
Actually running the 7-day plan this tool gave me, starting with getting that Experience entry live.

You can't optimize what isn't visible in the first place.
