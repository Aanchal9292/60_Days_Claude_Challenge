Loop Builder

What's a "loop prompt"? A normal prompt asks Claude once and takes whatever comes back. A loop prompt instructs Claude to draft an answer, judge its own draft against criteria you set, and revise — repeating until it meets a goal or hits a stop condition. Useful when "good enough on the first try" isn't good enough (e.g. a cover letter that must pass your own bar before you send it).
