Built: The Decision Strategist 🧭

Most career advice tools tell you what you want to hear. This one is designed to do the opposite — a 4-question AI interview that turns a fuzzy, anxious decision into a structured, data-backed report you can actually act on.

I used it on myself, live: "job now vs. more prep time." Watching my own reasoning get challenged instead of validated hit different.

✅ What I Built
✅ 4-question guided interview flow (one question at a time, no rushing)
✅ Auto-generated single-file interactive HTML dashboard as the output
✅ Animated decision matrix — 3 options scored across 7 dimensions with live-filling bars
✅ Built-in bias detector (named my own loss aversion and status quo bias, no mercy)
✅ Premortem section — forced me to imagine failure before committing
✅ 7-day test plan + a decisive, defendable verdict
✅ 3 shareable "screenshot cards" for socials, generated straight from my own answers

💡 Key Insight
The hardest decisions aren't usually missing information — they're missing permission. My "gut answer" showed up in question 3. Everything after that was just building the courage to trust it. A good decision tool doesn't hand you a new answer; it makes the one you already have undeniable.

🔜 What's Next
Testing this format with real users on harder, messier decisions — career, relationships, money — to see if the "challenge, don't comfort" framing holds up outside my own head.

Some tools are built to make you feel good. This one's built to make you honest with yourself — and honestly, that's rarer.
