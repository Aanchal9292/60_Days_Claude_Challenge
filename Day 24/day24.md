Project: Business Sustainability & Strategy Report Generator

Validation tells you if an idea is worth pursuing. An MVP blueprint tells you what to build first.

Neither one answers the only question that actually keeps a startup alive: will someone pay real money for this?

So today I built an AI Co-Founder that refuses to answer with vibes. It forces revenue over vanity metrics, distribution over features, and evidence over assumptions — then hands back a full business strategy report with an actual verdict.

What I Built:
✅ A Business Reality Check — who pays, why they pay, how they'll find you, and the weakest assumptions in the plan, before any strategy gets written
✅ A full Business Model Canvas + Revenue/Pricing/GTM strategy, built specifically around the institutional buyer, not the end user
✅ A Reverse SWOT — flipping "no users yet" and "huge market" to reveal the trap hiding inside each conventional strength
✅ An Investment Scorecard (0-100) across Viability, Revenue Potential, GTM, Competitive Strength, and Investor Readiness — with reasoning, not just numbers
✅ A one-page Visual Dashboard infographic pulling the entire business model, GTM flow, moat, risks, and scores into a single investor-style snapshot

Key Insight:
My "moat" scored 2-4 out of 10 today. That stung a little — until I realized the report was right to say it plainly instead of softening it. A founder who doesn't know their moat is weak can't go build one.

What's Next:
The report gave me one job before anything else: get one real institution to pay real money. Tomorrow's build is about testing that, not adding more features.

Final verdict: 🟡 Validate. Not invest. Not reject. Just — go prove it.
