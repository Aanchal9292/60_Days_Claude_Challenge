 Day 42/60: Your Personal Financial Command Center – Built by AI in Minutes!

What if you could have a fully personalized financial dashboard that understands your unique situation—without writing a single line of code?

I put Claude to the test to build a "Personal Financial Command Center" for a student with a part-time job and daily expense challenges.

After a short Q&A (Student → Part-time → Daily expense management → Auto-design), the AI generated a premium, self-contained HTML application packed with:

✅ Financial Health Score – A dynamic 0-100 score based on your savings rate, with a visual ring indicator.
✅ Interactive Charts – Pie chart for expense breakdown + bar chart comparing income vs. expenses over 3 months.
✅ Transaction Management – Add income (with source) and expenses (with categories like Rent, Groceries, Education).
✅ Budget Tracking – Set monthly budgets per category and watch live progress bars turn green/yellow/red.
✅ What-If Simulator – Adjust extra income or reduce expenses to see projected savings instantly.
✅ AI-Powered Insights – Personalized recommendations based on your spending patterns (e.g., "Your top spending category is Entertainment").
✅ Student-Specific Tips – Curated advice (50/30/20 rule, student discounts, emergency fund building).
✅ Local Storage – All your data persists after refresh.
✅ Dark/Light Mode & Print-Ready reports.

The best part? Zero external libraries – pure HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JS. The AI structured the entire UX from onboarding to actionable insights, tailored specifically for a student's financial reality.

This isn't just a tracker; it's a coach that helps you understand, manage, and improve your financial health.
