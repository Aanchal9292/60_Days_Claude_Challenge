Day 37/60 of building in public 🚀

Today's build: Task Compass — a management simulation game (not a quiz) that teaches how work actually moves through a startup.

Most "org chart" tools test whether you know job titles. Task Compass tests something more useful: do you understand ownership, delegation, collaboration, and escalation?

Three stages, each a little more realistic:

🎯 Stage 1 — Who Owns This? Drag the right role onto a real workplace ticket (like "checkout button broken on Safari") and see not just who owns it, but why — and who else typically assists.

🔀 Stage 2 — Task Routing Build the actual workflow a task follows through the org, step by step, then watch it animate through the company.

🤝 Stage 3 — Collaboration Challenge Messy, real situations — a sudden drop in customer satisfaction, a viral sales spike — where the right answer isn't one owner, it's a team. Pick up to 4 roles and see the full picture: primary owner, supporting teams, and the communication flow between them.

At the end, instead of a score, you get an honest reflection: where you understood ownership well, where you over-assigned responsibility, where you underestimated collaboration — plus one takeaway about how organizations really function.

Built as a single self-contained HTML file — no frameworks, no libraries, fully offline. Dark mode, glassmorphism, drag-and-drop, the whole game-feel package.

The bigger lesson I keep relearning building this: most interesting workplace problems aren't solved by finding "the right person." They're solved by finding the right team.
