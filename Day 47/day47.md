🚀 Day 47/60 — Built a Content Intelligence Studio!

Today, I built a Content Intelligence Studio designed to review resumes with an ATS-focused, keyword-precise approach. 📄🤖

The goal was simple: instead of just checking whether a resume “looks good,” the AI analyzes whether the content is actually relevant, keyword-aligned, and optimized for ATS/job-portal screening.

💡 The most valuable insight from the AI review:

A resume can contain strong technical skills and experience, but still perform poorly if the keywords and achievements are not aligned with the target role. The AI helped identify where the content could be more precise, relevant, and impact-oriented rather than simply adding more keywords.

This project was a great reminder that good content isn't just about what you know — it's also about how effectively you communicate it to both humans and AI systems.
