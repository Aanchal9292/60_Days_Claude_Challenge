Day 51/60 — #ABTalksAIChallenge
Starting a 10-day sprint: idea to deployed product, following a real SDLC
50 days of daily builds taught me how to ship fast. This capstone is testing something different — can I take one idea through Requirements → Design → Setup → Implementation → Testing → Deployment like an actual product cycle, not just a daily demo?
What I Built — Discovery & Planning :
 ✅ Ran a structured product discovery interview instead of jumping straight to code
 ✅ Landed on Readiness Score — a tool that gives final-year students an honest 0-100 competitiveness score across resume, LinkedIn, and mock interview defense, blended into one number
 ✅ Wrote a full PRD with an explicit "out of scope" list, not just a feature list
 ✅ Built a day-by-day implementation blueprint for all 9 remaining days, detailed enough that a fresh AI conversation could pick up building on any given day without re-deciding architecture
 ✅ Put together a pitch deck — Problem, Solution, Key Features, Technical Approach, Vision
Key Insight:
 The hardest decision today wasn't the idea — it was scope. I wanted resume + LinkedIn + interview defense all in one v1.0. The honest move was writing down exactly what gets simplified to make that possible in 9 days, and locking an explicit "will NOT build" list before writing a single line of code.
